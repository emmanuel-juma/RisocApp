Welcome to ResocApp

ResocApp is a platform that allows students and researchers to find resources in terms of Youtube videos, books and resourceful links.
Whether you study Engineering or researching about phylosophy and baking, you can do it with Resoc App. Discover resources, links and videos via ResocApp.


Our goal is to bring learning resources together in one place, with a simple design that makes it easy to research and find information.
